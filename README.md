# Harbor Lights — Open-World Crime in Solmar Bay

An original open-world crime game for make.fun gta6-01. You are Mara Voss,
a getaway driver in the fictional coastal city of Solmar Bay.

Play: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.

- Walk and drive a 3400x2600 city with districts, traffic and pedestrians
- Steal cars (E), drive-by shooting, combat on foot
- Wanted stars (0-5) with SBPD police pursuit, escape and busted fines
- Six story missions with dialogue, objectives and cash rewards
- HUD with minimap, health/armor, cash, speed and mission tracker
- Day/night cycle, rain weather, particles, screen shake
- Synthesized WebAudio SFX and procedural music (M to mute)
- Autosave to localStorage; pause (P), restart (R)

Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse aim, click shoot, E enter/exit car,
F interact, Space handbrake, Shift sprint, P pause, M mute.

All art, names, story, sound and music are original. No assets from any
commercial game. MIT licensed.
